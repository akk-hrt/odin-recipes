Creamy Corn Soup

Description

Let's create a super easy corn soup.

Ingredients

  • onion
  • fresh parsely
  • butter
  • garlic
  • all-purpose flour
  • milk
  • chicken broth
  • cans of whole kernel corn
  • cream cheese
  • garlic salt
  • ground black pepper

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Cook and stir onion, parsely, butter and minced garlic in a large pot over medium heat for 5-10 minutes
  3. Whisk in flour, milk and chicken broth until soup thickens slightly.
  4. Stir in corn and cream cheese until heated through.
  5. Add garlic salt and black pepper. Stir until combined.
