Lasagna

Description

Let's create a super easy lasagna.

Ingredients

  • Beef
  • Spaghetti sauce of your choice
  • Cheeses of your choice
  • Eggs
  • Seasonings of your choice
  • Lasagna noodles
  • water

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Preheat the oven.
  3. Heat a wok over medium-high heat.
  4. Cook and stir beef in the wok until browned.
  5. Drain and discard grease.
  6. Stir in spaghetti sauce and simmer.
  7. Spread the sauce over a baking dish.
  8. Cover with uncooked lasagna noodles and cheese of your choice.
  9. Cover the baking dish with aluminium foil tightly. Bake in the preheated oven for 60 minutes.
