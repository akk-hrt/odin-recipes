Description

Let's create a super easy fruit salad.

Ingredients

  • strawberries
  • grapes
  • kiwi fruits
  • bananas
  • peaches

Steps

  1. Clean the strawberries.
  2. Remove seeds from the grapes wherever necessary.
  3. Peel and slice the kiwi fruits.
  4. Slice the bananas.
  5. Cut peaches.
  6. Combine all the fruits in a large bowl and mix gently.
  7. Chill the mixed fruits for 1 hour.
